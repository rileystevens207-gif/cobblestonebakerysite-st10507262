Our Story

Cobblestone Bakery opened its doors in 2015 in Cape Town, started by a small team who believed the city deserved honest, from-scratch baking. What began as a single oven and a market stall has grown into a neighbourhood favourite for bread, pastries, and celebration cakes.

Our Mission

To bring genuine, handmade baking back to the everyday table — using quality local ingredients and traditional methods, without shortcuts.

Our Vision

To be Cape Town's go-to bakery for both daily bread and once-a-year celebration cakes, known for consistency and care in every bake.

Meet the Team

  • Head Baker: Oversees all bread and pastry production.
  • Cake Decorator: Designs and builds custom celebration cakes.
  • Front of House: Manages orders and customer enquiries.
